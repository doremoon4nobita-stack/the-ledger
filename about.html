About The Ledger

The Ledger is a small site with one goal: explain personal finance the way it should have been explained the first time — in plain language, with real numbers, and without anything to sell you.

Most financial content online is written to sell a product or chase clicks. We're trying something simpler: guides you'd actually want to send to a friend who's asking "how do I even start with a budget?"

This site is run independently and is not affiliated with any bank, lender, or financial product. Nothing here is personalized financial advice — for decisions specific to your situation, a licensed financial advisor is worth talking to.
